Bitcoin — The Truth Behind the Narrative

What the market doesn’t want you to know.

Bitcoin is sold to the world as a promise of financial freedom, a store of value, privacy, and independence from governments. But... is it really?

This book offers a direct, critical, and well-founded analysis of what lies behind the storytelling that turned Bitcoin into a global phenomenon. Understand why it doesn’t work as money, fails as a store of value, and survives, in practice, as an instrument of extreme speculation.

With accessible language, real-world examples, and undeniable facts — from Silk Road to the Bitfinex case, including scams, fraud, tracking, and corporate narratives — this book demystifies Bitcoin and reveals its true nature in the global economic landscape.

This is not a book against technology or the crypto market. It’s an invitation to reflection, critical thinking, and financial responsibility. Because before you invest, you need to truly understand what you’re investing in.

If you’re looking for knowledge, clarity, and a way to escape misinformation, this book is for you.
