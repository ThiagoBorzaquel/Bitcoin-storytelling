Bitcoin — The Truth Behind the Narrative

What the market doesn’t want you to know.

Bitcoin is sold as a promise of financial freedom, a store of value, privacy, and independence from governments. This book offers a direct, critical, and well-founded analysis of the storytelling behind that global phenomenon.

Read description Português Español

Description

Understand why it doesn’t work as money, fails as a store of value, and survives in practice as an instrument of extreme speculation.

Facts

Silk Road, Bitfinex, scams, fraud, tracking, and corporate narratives are included in the analysis.

Reading

An invitation to reflection, critical thinking, and financial responsibility.

Synopsis

This is not a book against technology or the crypto market. It asks readers to understand what they are investing in before they invest.
